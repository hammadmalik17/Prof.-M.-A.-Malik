About

Scholar. Teacher.
Public intellectual.

An economist who has spent over two decades connecting classroom rigour with the questions that matter to Telangana's people.

Trained at Osmania University (Ph.D. 2008, with an LL.B. alongside), his research covers inclusive development, minority empowerment, agriculture and land use, MGNREGS, GST and MSMEs, and the political economy of Telangana. He has written on Economics in the Telugu daily Andhra Jyothi for 24 years.

He is the author of 30 books, including textbooks and competitive-exam guides, with chapters for Telugu Akademi and Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Open University. He has presented at 32 seminars and conferences, as author, resource person and session chair.
